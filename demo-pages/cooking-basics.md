# Cooking Basics

Fundamental techniques for preparing food at home.

## Heat Transfer

There are three primary methods of heat transfer in cooking: conduction (direct contact with a hot surface), convection (hot air or liquid circulation), and radiation (infrared energy from a grill or broiler).

## Timing

Most vegetables cook in 5–12 minutes depending on density. Root vegetables take longer than leafy greens. Proteins need internal temperature verification rather than time alone. The recommended safe internal temperature for poultry is 82 °C.
