# Nutrition

How food composition affects health and energy.

## Macronutrients

Proteins, fats, and carbohydrates provide energy measured in calories. Protein and carbohydrates yield roughly 4 kcal per gram; fat yields about 9 kcal per gram.

## Vitamins

Vitamins are organic compounds required in small amounts. They are classified as fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) or water-soluble (B-complex, C). Water-soluble vitamins are easily lost during cooking.
